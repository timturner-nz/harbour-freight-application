# Business Rules

Capture the rules that make the system behave correctly.

## Categories to document

### Validation rules
What input is allowed or rejected.

### Lifecycle rules
What states exist and what transitions are allowed.

### Permissions rules
Who can see or do what.

### Calculation rules
How totals, prices, dates, risk scores, or other derived values are determined.

### Audit rules
What must be recorded for traceability.

### Edge cases
Known special cases that must not be broken.

## Writing guidance

- Write rules as testable statements.
- Avoid vague language such as "usually" or "normally" unless that ambiguity is deliberate.
- Prefer explicit examples where the rule is subtle.
